## 1. The Core Essence: Ajmaq & Cib
The 13th symbol of the Mayan calendar carries two primary linguistic and symbolic layers:

* **Ajmaq (K’iche’):** Known as the **"Day of Pardon."** It is associated with the **Vulture**, the cleanser of the soul. It represents the ability to transform "errors" or past mistakes into spiritual fuel.
* **Cib (Yucatec):** Translated as **"Wax" or "Candle."** It represents the **Inner Light** and the divine spark. It is the smoke of incense that carries prayers to the ancestors.
* **The Label "Wisdom":** This sign represents the **"Old Soul."** True wisdom is defined here as the result of making mistakes, seeking forgiveness, and lighting the candle of experience to see through the darkness.
